About LONGAFTERNOON

LongAfternoon is a mysterious independent musical artist/project (also known by the name Yani) known for calm, acoustic, or lofi-style melodies and songs like "Painted Silence" and "Unrestricted" on Spotify and other mainstream music platforms, who keeps a low profile without showing their face.

Even though we have very little information about this artist we do know that Longafternoon is related to the korean manhwa series called "My Bias gets on the last train".
